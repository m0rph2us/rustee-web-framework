Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0037: Kafka Event Delivery Observability

Status: Accepted

Context

Kafka delivery settlement needs observable success, retry, DLQ, and cancellation behavior while avoiding sensitive stream detail in metrics.

Decision

Use a provider-neutral delivery observer and optional collector for bounded provider/outcome, in-flight count, and global duration. Finish acknowledged, retried, or dead-lettered only after source settlement.

Consequences

Topics, partitions, groups, offsets, keys, payloads, endpoints, and raw errors are not exporter labels. Lag diagnostics and alert policy remain application-owned.
