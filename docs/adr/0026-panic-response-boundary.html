Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0026: Panic Response Boundary

Status: Accepted

Context

Handlers may panic, but exposing panic payloads or silently changing process policy is unsafe.

Decision

Provide an opt-in panic-catching layer that converts a handler panic before response commit into a fixed redacted 500 response.

Consequences

The layer does not change panic hooks, restart policy, or post-commit body panic behavior. Applications own incident logging, supervision, and recovery.
