Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0024: AI Batch Artifact Reconciliation Boundary

Status: Accepted

Context

A completed batch artifact can contain partial, duplicated, or ambiguous domain effects and must not be automatically interpreted as business success.

Decision

Use reference-only reconciliation jobs keyed by scope, catalog, run, artifact role, file ID, and reconciliation key; reserve once, process under authorization, then record exact reconciliation.

Consequences

Pending artifacts remain ambiguous, not retryable by default. Domain row idempotency, retention/deletion, partial-result policy, and manual recovery stay application-owned.
