Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0021: AI Response Cache Boundary

Status: Accepted

Context

AI response caching can reduce cost but can cross tenant, retention, tool, and stale-answer boundaries.

Decision

Use an application-generated tenant-scoped opaque SHA-256 exact key, explicit TTL of at most 24 hours, exact invalidation, bounded serialization, and tool-interaction exclusion.

Consequences

Fingerprint/salt/version strategy, retention, tenant erase, outage behavior, stale UX, encryption, and stampede control remain application and deployment policy.
