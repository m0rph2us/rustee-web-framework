Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0033: Recurring Job IANA Time-Zone Calendar

Status: Accepted

Context

Business schedules are often expressed in a named civil time zone, where daylight-saving gaps and overlaps cannot be modeled safely with a fixed offset.

Decision

Store an explicit durable IANA time zone, defaulting existing schedules to UTC. Skip forward DST gaps and stage only the earlier UTC occurrence of a backward overlap.

Consequences

Time-zone definition drift is rejected. Holiday calendars, catch-up policy, and tzdb update qualification remain application and deployment responsibilities.
