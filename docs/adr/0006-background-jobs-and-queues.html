ADR 0006: Background Jobs and Queue Boundary

Status: Accepted

Context

Background work needs retries, DLQs, graceful drain, and provider-specific acknowledgement semantics without a false exactly-once abstraction.

Decision

Provide typed job envelopes, immutable registries, explicit retry policy, provider delivery attempts, at-least-once workers, and focused NATS, Redis Streams, RabbitMQ, and SQS adapters. Use PostgreSQL outbox/inbox helpers for durable staging.

Consequences

Handlers require idempotency. Provider topology, credentials, replay, supervision, external effects, and operational alerting remain application and deployment responsibilities.
