Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0013: Release Tracks and Support Policy

Status: Accepted

Context

Core and optional adapters have different maturity, provider, and operational evidence.

Decision

Use separate core and integration release tracks with Experimental, Supported, and Enterprise-qualified levels. Promotion requires the documented automated, operational, and external evidence.

Consequences

Support claims remain granular and reversible. Inventory, release notes, migration guidance, support matrix, and qualification register must change with a promotion or downgrade.
