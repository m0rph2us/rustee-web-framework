Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0029: PostgreSQL Recurring Job Scheduler Boundary

Status: Accepted

Context

Recurring jobs need durable schedule state and atomic materialization with business-delivery infrastructure rather than in-memory timers.

Decision

Register typed recurring jobs in PostgreSQL. An application-owned supervisor claims due schedules with SKIP LOCKED, stages a fresh outbox job, and advances the next occurrence in one transaction.

Consequences

Missed ticks are not automatically replayed in bulk. Holiday calendars, tenant fairness, quotas, topology, and supervisor lifecycle remain application responsibilities.
