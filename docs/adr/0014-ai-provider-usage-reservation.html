Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0014: AI Provider Usage Reservation

Status: Accepted

Context

AI requests can incur provider cost and must not bypass tenant, budget, or audit policy through retries or multiple pipeline paths.

Decision

Reserve a content-free provider attempt before dispatch and settle observed usage afterward through explicit AiUsageReservation and ledger contracts.

Consequences

Pricing, quotas, refunds, provider reconciliation, and cross-service atomicity remain application policy. A reservation does not expose prompt content or guarantee provider billing equivalence.
