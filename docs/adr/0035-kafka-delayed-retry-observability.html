Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0035: Kafka Delayed-Retry Observability

Status: Accepted

Context

The delayed-retry relay needs visibility without turning topics, offsets, payloads, endpoints, or storage errors into sensitive high-cardinality metrics.

Decision

Emit cancellation-safe pass outcomes, confirmed successful-pass count, and global duration. Applications may explicitly record the relay's aggregate unpublished/due/leased/oldest-due backlog.

Consequences

Collector and Prometheus helper remain exporter-neutral and route-free. Supervision, alerting, backlog interpretation, and incident response remain application-owned.
