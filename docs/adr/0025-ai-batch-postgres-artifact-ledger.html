Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0025: AI Batch PostgreSQL Artifact Ledger

Status: Accepted

Context

Reference-only artifact reconciliation needs durable concurrent reservation and a way to preserve ambiguity after partial work or crashes.

Decision

Provide an optional PostgreSQL ledger keyed by scope and reconciliation key with exact identity conflict rejection, atomic reservation, idempotent completion, and bounded pending query.

Consequences

Deployment owns migration, RLS, backups, pending recovery, row-level domain ledger, and retention. Ledger completion is not provider deletion or billing settlement.
