Architecture Decision Record

ADR 0031: PostgreSQL Scheduler Pass Observability Boundary

Status: Accepted

Context

Recurring scheduler passes need operational signals without leaking schedule keys, expressions, tenants, destinations, payloads, or storage errors through metrics.

Decision

Expose a cancellation-safe fire observer and optional exporter-neutral collector for fixed terminal outcomes, aggregate claimed/staged/rate-limited counts, and global duration.

Consequences

Prometheus text export is explicit and creates no registry or route. Scheduling frequency, alerts, labels, retention, and application-specific diagnosis remain deployment-owned.
