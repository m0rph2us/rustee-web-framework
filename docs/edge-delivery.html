Edge Delivery Policy

A CDN Is an Explicit Deployment Contract, Not Rustee Transport

Rustee runs at the origin. TLS termination, proxy identity, caching, compression, purge, and origin draining are owned and qualified by the deployment.

Default Decision

  • Terminate public TLS at the approved edge or ingress and configure origin trust only for known edge peer CIDRs.
  • Overwrite client-supplied forwarded identity at ingress before Rustee restores a trusted normalized representation.
  • Define cache keys, headers, variation, TTL, stale behavior, compression, and purge in the edge configuration and application response policy.
  • Do not treat local origin tests as evidence for public CDN correctness.

Responsibility Split

Rustee origin Deployment edge
Routes, response headers, origin compression/static behavior, bounded proxy trust TLS, DNS, WAF, CDN rules, cache key, purge, certificate lifecycle, peer addresses, and global routing
Application health and graceful drain signals Traffic shift, origin removal, regional failover, and incident response

Cache, Compression, and Change Control

  • Keep compressed and identity responses separate with Vary: Accept-Encoding. The edge must preserve that variation.
  • Do not cache authenticated, tenant-specific, or mutable responses unless an explicit reviewed policy defines the key and invalidation behavior.
  • Record cache-rule, compression, purge, origin, certificate, and proxy-trust changes with owner, validation, rollback, and incident impact.
  • Use a controlled purge procedure and verify the expected representation before and after the change.

Promotion Evidence

Supported edge delivery requires an owner-approved deployment record showing TLS and certificate handling, trusted-proxy behavior, cache-variant isolation, compression behavior, purge verification, origin drain, rollback, observability, and an outage or recovery exercise. Rustee does not provide a generic CDN adapter or claim multi-provider edge compatibility without that evidence.
